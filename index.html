01 — ABOUT

From operations floor to product analyst.

Tatev is an IT Business Analyst at Ameriabank, working on MyBusiness — a platform that lets individual entrepreneurs and LLCs apply for banking products, including payment instruments, loans, account opening and payroll, without a branch visit. Before Ameriabank she spent nearly three years in the IT industry: first as Program Manager running B2B programs at Armenian Code Academy, then as IT Project Manager at WeemTech.

Her path started on a bank's operations floor, as a Customer Due Diligence intern at HSBC — and a Master's in Strategic Management, awarded with honors, gave that early instinct a framework for coordinating cross-functional teams and delivering projects from A to Z.
